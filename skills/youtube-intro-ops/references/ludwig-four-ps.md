## Source-derived mechanics

Paraphrase the lesson as:

- **Premise:** explain the video's context immediately; it functions as the
  hook.
- **Problem:** establish the conflict and stakes the video must resolve.
- **Plug:** use the intro as an optional call-to-action moment, but do not
  treat the plug as required.
- **Payoff:** tease what the viewer will receive after following the premise
  and problem through the video.
- **Voice:** keep the creator's natural delivery instead of copying a generic
  high-energy YouTube tone.

Ludwig presents the intro as part of packaging alongside the idea, title, and
thumbnail. Treat that as a consistency requirement: the opening must deliver
the experience the packaging led the viewer to expect.

## Attribution boundary

Call this the **four Ps**, not the three Ps. Do not conflate it with the
unrelated Proof-Promise-Plan framework or with Promise-Progress-Payoff.

Use the framework without copying Ludwig's identity, exact wording, jokes, or
delivery style.
