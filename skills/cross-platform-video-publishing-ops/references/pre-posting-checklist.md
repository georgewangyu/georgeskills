# Pre-posting packet

Use for a finished video being prepared for review, including posts with no
resource CTA. Keep creator accounts, approved copy conventions, preferences
and per-post receipts in the injected private creator/project layer.

## Freeze the candidate

Record the current media path, independently calculated hash, duration and
approval state. Read its current transcript/captions and lineage. A newer
export supersedes an old posting packet. Preparing a packet does not authorize
changing or rendering the video, or declare it creator-approved.

## Caption and promise

Load the creator's voice and applicable series/platform profile. Inspect prior
approved packets and published copy. Draft the actual caption, with variants
only where the platform requires different delivery wording.

Extract the exact spoken, burned-in and caption CTAs. Build a compact mapping:
`promise -> available resource -> destination -> fulfillment channel -> state`.
Distinguish public documentation, downloadable artifacts, private helpers,
failed experiments and incidental examples. Open every destination and inspect
its content and availability, not only its HTTP status. Do not promise a
private helper, missing download or an earlier episode's resource list.

If a promise cannot be met, finish independent prep and ask only for the
substantive choice that remains. Record the release hold. Do not silently
rewrite locked speech or invent an asset. Normal profile/support links require
an established creator practice or explicit choice, plus URL verification.

## Creator review before campaign entry

Before entering a proposed resource offer into ManyChat or another delivery
provider, present the creator with the exact caption, complete resource list,
DM text, button labels/URLs and proposed targeting. Obtain explicit approval
for that version before creating, duplicating or editing even an inactive
campaign. Read-only account inspection and local drafts may proceed first.
Creator-requested edits to the reviewed proposal are part of that approval
when the requested final copy is unambiguous. Apply those edits without asking
for the same review again. New substantive choices still require review.
Approval can cover both entry and Go Live when the creator explicitly requests
both for the reviewed offer and next-post audience. Draft-only approval remains
limited to draft entry.

## Resource delivery, when needed

Choose an existing public resource or direct links first. A new companion page
is optional, not a prerequisite for a short list. Use a companion-page workflow
when explanation or resource navigation actually needs a page. A local draft
is not an audience-accessible URL. Deployment requires its own authorization.

Only prepare ManyChat when the post promises that fulfillment path. Load
`manychat-automation-ops` for account inspection, campaign reuse and configuration.
Reuse its procedure instead of maintaining a second funnel recipe here. Inspect
current source-flow edit settings and active overlaps. After the creator
review above, create a distinct inactive copy, then adapt the actual resource copy and destinations. Keep
source flows intact and targeting bounded to the intended post or an inactive
next-post draft. Never expand to all posts, ordinary DMs or story replies just
to make a comment promise work.

## Saved-state verification

Reload the saved inactive campaign and inspect its exact identity, targeting,
matching mode, opener/button, optional steps, full delivery text and each saved
button URL. Note preview truncation separately from stored text. Preserve
provider timing or follow-check limitations rather than inventing a test pass.
A local recipe, preview, saved draft, active campaign and successful delivery
are distinct states. No recipient test or send occurs without authorization.
For a post without a resource CTA, mark this lane not applicable.

## Authorized pre-post activation

When the creator approves the reviewed resource offer and explicitly asks to
Go Live for the upcoming post, save and verify the campaign, check competing
next-post reservations and overlapping responders, then activate it before the
media is posted. Do not wait for a Reel ID or ask for activation approval again.
Keep any-word matching bounded to the one next post, never all posts or DMs.
Verify Live status and next-post scope after reload. Report it as active and
awaiting the next post, not as delivery-tested or already bound to the Reel.
After the approved Reel is published, verify exact-post binding. If a different
post will go first, stop/re-scope the reservation within existing authority
before it can attach to the wrong post. Activation does not authorize uploading
or publishing the video, or sending a test message.

## Handoff before release

Return the actual caption and a short ready/missing checklist, linking the
review packet and campaign draft when present. Include:

- exact media identity and creator approval status
- verified promise/resource mapping and any unresolved resource choice
- delivery configuration state, targeting, overlap and test limits
- separate outstanding approvals for copy/media, activation and publication
- launch order: approved fulfillment first when using next-post delivery,
  media release, then verify binding to the exact new post

Recheck next-post reservations immediately before authorized activation. If
another post may intervene, keep the draft stopped and bind the intended exact
post later. An inactive draft is review-ready, not delivery-ready. Close with
private evidence pointers and the smallest concrete next action.
