## Decision Gate

Before a consequential commitment, ask:

1. Does this align with the life and contribution described here?
2. What durable asset, capability, relationship, or understanding compounds?
3. Would the work still have substance if nobody were impressed?
4. If it succeeds unusually well, do I want the obligations and identity that
   follow?
5. What health, relationship, meaningful work, integrity, or freedom loses
   time if I say yes?
6. Is this a destination, bridge, experiment, or proxy?
7. What evidence would cause me to reconsider?
