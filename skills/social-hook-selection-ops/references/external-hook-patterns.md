# External Hook Patterns

Use these as `source-observed` or `candidate` hook families when a local
workspace has no stronger owned-account pattern. Adapt the structure, not the
source creator's identity.

## Hidden Cost Reversal

```text
Everyone tells you [obvious advice], but no one tells you [hidden cost / lonely truth].
```

Good for career, creator, AI workflow, money, and life-tradeoff videos where
the second half can reveal a concrete tension.

## Blunt Reality Confession

```text
Let's come clean about the reality of [thing].
```

Good for videos with receipts, corrections, or insider honesty. Pair it with a
visible proof object so it does not become generic commentary.

## Credibility Stack

```text
My credibility? [credential 1], [credential 2], [credential 3].
```

Good when the viewer needs fast trust before a teaching video. Use sparingly,
and show the proof visually when possible.

## Explain It Simply

```text
[Thing] explained to a 5-year-old.
```

Good for dense topics that can be made simpler without dumbing them down.

## Emerging Realization

```text
People are beginning to realize that [weird / pattern-interrupt statement].
```

Good for counterintuitive theses where the body can make the weird statement
feel obvious by the end.

## Timed Outcome Challenge

```text
Is it possible to [outcome] in [extremely short time frame]?
```

Good for mini experiments, build-in-public videos, learning challenges, and
proof-sequence content with a clear clock.
