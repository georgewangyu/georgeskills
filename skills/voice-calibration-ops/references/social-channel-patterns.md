# Social Channel Patterns

Use this only when the output is a social post or thread.

## X

- Lead with the concrete thought, not a platform preamble.
- Keep the first post link-free unless the user asks otherwise.
- Make each post stand on its own enough to be readable without clicking.
- Prefer one strong claim over a polished multi-point announcement.
- Put supporting links, product links, or footers in a later reply when that
  helps reach or readability.

## LinkedIn

- The first two lines carry most of the post. Name the concrete change or
  tension there.
- Professional does not mean inflated. Use the writer's normal confidence,
  not generic expertise performance.
- Use bullets for release notes or scannable lists. Use prose for opinions,
  lessons, and story-driven posts.
- A launch post should answer:
  - what changed
  - why the change matters in concrete terms
  - who can use it
  - what proof or artifact exists
  - what the reader can do next

## Hook Set

For social drafting, produce 2-3 options before choosing one:

- Direct hook: names the concrete update.
- Tension hook: names the problem or failure mode.
- Personal/process hook: names what changed in the builder's understanding.

Then choose one based on the source voice profile.
